Three habits that cook your brain and how to uncook it from a licensed therapist. Number one, endless scrolling. When you constantly consume fast-paced, short-form content, your brain becomes accustomed to receiving something new every few seconds. Number three, constantly multitasking. When you repeatedly switch between your phone, work, conversations, and entertainment, your brain never fully settles into one activity. This can leave you feeling mentally scattered and exhausted, even when you have not accomplished very much. To uncook your brain, practice doing one thing at a time. Put your phone away while you work. Finish one task before starting another. and allow yourself to experience moments without additional stimulation. Do this and your brain will thank you for it.
