5 Signs Your Mental Health Is Getting Cooked From A Licensed Therapist 1. You Are More Irritable One of the biggest signs I look for when someone's mental health is getting worse is how easily they snap at small things. Usually this happens when somebody has been mentally overwhelmed for a long time and they are reaching their limit. Little inconveniences suddenly feel huge and their frustration comes out faster. Increased irritability is often a strong sign that somebody is struggling emotionally. Number two is constant fatigue. When mental health declines, even basic day-to-day life can start feeling exhausting. Getting out of bed can feel extremely hard. Small responsibilities feel overwhelming. It can feel like your body and mind are dragging through the day with no energy left. And obviously, physical conditions should always be ruled out. But when everything feels mentally exhausting, that is often a sign your mental health is getting worse. Number three, losing interest in things you used to enjoy. Hobbies stop feeling enjoyable. Nothing is giving you joy anymore. Things that once helped you feel alive suddenly feel flat. And when nothing really pulls you into a better headspace anymore, that is usually a sign that something deeper is going on mentally. Number four is major changes in appetite. Some people barely eat when they're struggling mentally because their appetite completely disappears. Other people overeat because food becomes a way to cope with stress or emotional pain. Significant weight loss or weight gain in a short period of time, especially when it is unintentional, can be a major warning sign that somebody is not doing well mentally. And number five is major changes in sleep. Sleeping all day, barely sleeping, waking up constantly through the night, staying up until 4am because your brain will not shut off Those are all signs that something could be going on mentally

Второй признак - постоянная усталость. Когда психическое здоровье ухудшается, даже базовая повседневная жизнь может начать казаться изнурительной. Вставать с постели может быть очень трудно.
