15 powerful sentences by Robert Greene that will change how you view the world:

Robert Greene is one of my favorite authors. I first discovered him when a friend gifted me The 48 Laws of Power years back. It was sharp, unflinching, and profoundly insightful. It completely changed how I view power dynamics, strategy, and human behavior. From there, I dove more into his other works: The Laws of Human Nature, Mastery, The Daily Laws, and more.

What sets Greene writing apart is his masterful ability to weave together deep historical research, psychology, philosophy, and timeless wisdom into practical, actionable guidance. Drawing from figures like Machiavelli, Sun Tzu, Queen Elizabeth I, and countless others, he distills centuries of strategic thinking into modern lessons on influence, seduction, self-mastery, and personal power.

Here are some of Robert Greene’s books you can pick up:

- The 48 Laws of Power
- The Laws of Human Nature 
- Mastery 
- The 33 Strategies of War
