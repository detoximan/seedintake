3 – Текст под медиа:
In these lines, the poet expresses a quiet, selfless farewell filled with pain and dignity. He assures the beloved that he will not make things harder for them — he will leave without creating trouble, hiding his tears within his eyes. This reflects a love that chooses the other’s ease over its own suffering.
He then makes a gentle request: to let him remain for a little while at the doorstep, as if gathering himself. It shows his emotional state — overwhelmed, needing a moment before he can truly walk away. Through these soft yet deeply moving words, the poet beautifully captures the grace of leaving with love intact, even when the heart is breaking.

POET - Amjad Islam Amjad (1944-2023) was a renowned Pakistani Urdu poet, playwright, and columnist known for his deep, humanistic, and emotionally rich writing. Over his long career, he authored more than 40 books of poetry, criticism, and drama, and gained massive popularity for his iconic TV drama series “Waris”, which became a classic in Pakistan. He received numerous honors, including the Sitara-i-Imtiaz, Pride of Performance, and several national literary awards for his contribution to Urdu literature. His famous works include his poetry collections “Barzakh,” “Fishaar,” “Satvan Dar,” “Phir Yun Hua,” and his celebrated drama writings. Amjad’s writing is admired for its simplicity, philosophical depth, and the way it touches everyday human emotions, making him one of the most respected literary voices in modern Urdu literature.
