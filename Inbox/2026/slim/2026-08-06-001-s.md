Fyodor Dostoevsky

Russian novelist and philosopher known for exploring psychology, suffering, faith, guilt, and human nature. His works often dive deep into the emotional and moral struggles of people.
Famous books include Crime and Punishment, The Brothers Karamazov, and Notes from Underground.

Franz Kafka

A German-speaking writer famous for themes of isolation, anxiety, absurdity, and feeling trapped in life. His writing feels deeply personal, surreal, and emotionally haunting.
Famous works include The Metamorphosis, The Trial, and The Castle.

They are often compared because both wrote about loneliness, suffering, and the darker side of the human mind — but Dostoevsky focused more on emotional and spiritual conflict, while Kafka focused on alienation and existential fear.
