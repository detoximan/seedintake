5 ways to become less annoying from a licensed therapist. Number 1. Don't make every conversation about yourself. When someone constantly redirects conversations back to themselves, people start feeling unimportant. Sometimes people do this because they're trying to relate, which is understandable. But when every story somehow becomes about you, people stop feeling listened to and start feeling dismissed. Number 2. Don't get super defensive. If every single piece of feedback turns into an excuse or an argument, people eventually stop being honest with you. Constructive feedback and even some criticism is not always an attack. Sometimes people are simply trying to improve the relationship or communicate something important to you. Number three, stop drilling people after they apologize. If someone sincerely apologizes and you accept it, but then you keep hammering them over the mistake afterward, it will become exhausting and very annoying. And yes, of course, it's important to address problems. But if people feel like apologizing never actually ends the conflict, they actually become less likely to take accountability in the future. Number four, stop constantly complaining. Fenton sometimes is healthy. Talking about problems is normal. But if almost every interaction revolves around negativity, people start associating you with emotional exhaustion. Nobody wants to leave the conversations feeling drained every single time. Number five, stop one-upping people's negative emotions. If someone opens up about something painful and your immediate reaction is to explain how you had it worse, it can make the other person feel completely invalidated. Most of the time people are not looking for comparison. They just want to feel heard and emotionally supported in that moment.

Если каждая отзыв становится оправданием или спором, люди в конечном итоге перестают быть честными с вами. Конструктивная обратная связь и даже некоторая критика не всегда является атакой. Иногда люди просто пытаются улучшить отношения или передать что-то важное вам. Номер три, перестаньте допрашивать людей после того, как они извинились.
