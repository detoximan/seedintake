You have felt every one of these. You just never knew they had a name. Sonder, that moment you look at a stranger and realize their life is just as full and deep as yours. Limerence, when your mind builds a whole life with someone you barely know and you cannot make it stop. Anticipatory grief, missing someone who is still right here because you can already feel the goodbye coming. Depersonalization, when you feel far from yourself like you are watching your own life through glass. Saudade, a soft ache for a person, a place, or a time you are not sure you will ever get back. The Sunday dread, that quiet weight that creeps in before the week has even started. There is comfort in this. The feeling was never too much. It just needed a name.
