Three habits that cook your brain and how to uncook it from a licensed therapist. Number one, endless scrolling. When you constantly consume fast-paced, short-form content, your brain becomes accustomed to receiving something new every few seconds. This can make slower activities feel boring and sustained focus more difficult. To uncook your brain, regularly engage in activities that require your attention for longer periods, such as reading, making arts, completing a puzzle, or watching a full movie without checking your phone. Consistently practicing sustained focus can help rebuild your attention span Number two, staying inside all day Your brain benefits from movement, sunlight, and new experiences When you spend the entire day inside, the hours can start blending together And life may begin to feel very repetitive To uncook your brain, step outside and reconnect with the world around you Take a walk, visit somewhere new, or simply sit outside without looking at your phone A change of environment can help you feel more alert and much more present. Number three, constantly multitasking. When you repeatedly switch between your phone, work, conversations, and entertainment, your brain never fully settles into one activity. This can leave you feeling mentally scattered and exhausted, even when you have not accomplished very much. To uncook your brain, practice doing one thing at a time. Put your phone away while you work. Finish one task before starting another. and allow yourself to experience moments without additional stimulation. Do this and your brain will thank you for it.
