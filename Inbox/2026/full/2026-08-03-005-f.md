We are all broken, that’s how the light gets in. The fire was always burning within me but the flames were busy keeping everyone else warm. Between what is said and not meant, and what is meant and not said, most of love is lost. What are you afraid of losing when nothing in this world truly belongs to you. We thank some people for merely living at the same time as we do. To whom do I owe the biggest apology? No one’s been crueler than I’ve been to me. If a million loved you I am one of them, and if one loved you it was me, if no one loved you then know that I am dead. Like a compass needle always points north, a man’s accusing finger always finds a woman. People, people, people, an ocean of people, but I can’t find even a drop of humanity. The seats are empty, the theatre is dark, why do you keep acting. It’s easier to fool people than to convince them they’ve been fooled.
