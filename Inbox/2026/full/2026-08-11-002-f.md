Four ways to become more emotionally secure from a licensed therapist. Number one, accept the possibility of suffering. One of the biggest causes of chronic anxiety is the belief that we must prevent ourselves from even feeling emotional pain. So anxiety steps in and tries to protect us. It makes us overthink, seek reassurance, replay conversations, prepare for every possible outcome. But emotionally secure people eventually realize that no matter how careful they are, discomfort is still a part of life. And the moment you stop treating emotional pain like something that must be eliminated at all costs, your anxiety starts to chill out. Number two, learn the difference between influence and control. A lot of suffering comes from trying to control things that were never fully controllable to begin with. Control means guaranteeing an outcome. Influence means increasing the likelihood of an outcome. You can influence how you communicate with someone. You cannot control how they respond. You can influence your effort. You cannot control every result. Emotionally secure people stop measuring their worth only by outcomes. They start paying attention to whether they handle situations in alignment with the person they want to be Number three learn to sit with uncomfortable feelings A lot of unhealthy behaviors are actually attempts to escape emotions People do not always avoid situations. Sometimes they are avoiding what they might feel in those situations. Rejection, embarrassment, sadness, anger. One of the healthiest things you can do is stop treating emotions like emergencies. So instead of immediately trying to escape what you're feeling, validate it. It's okay for me to feel anxious. It's okay for me to feel embarrassed. I want you to know that emotional security is not becoming emotionless. It's learning that you can experience difficult emotions without collapsing because of them. Number four, turn demands into preferences. A lot of anxiety comes from demanding statements. I must succeed. This cannot go wrong. They have to understand me. I should never make mistakes. The problem with demands is that they create rigid pressure around how life is supposed to go. And when reality does not cooperate, your mental health suffers. A healthier approach is learning to change these demands into preferences. I want this to happen. I hope things go well. You are still allowed to care deeply about something without demanding that life unfolds exactly the way you expected to.
