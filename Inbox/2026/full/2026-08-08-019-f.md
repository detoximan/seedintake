10 out of 10 signs of high intelligence, but they become progressively more niche. They can take a complex concept and translate it into a very simple analogy on the fly without losing that concept's underlying truths. In other words, they can explain a PhD concept in a way that a first grader would understand with a high degree of accuracy. Cognitive flexibility.
